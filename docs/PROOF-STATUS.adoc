== Vocabulary

Closed set, `+PROOF-POLICY.adoc+` §5. Machine-readable ledgers use the
lowercase token; prose may use the label.

[width="100%",cols="22%,16%,62%",options="header"]
|===
|Token |Label |Means

|`+proven+` |PROVEN
|Machine-checked with no escape hatch, trust boundary named. A bounded check is
not this row.

|`+proven-with-postulates+` |PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS
|A kernel checked it and it rests on named necessary axioms, listed here.

|`+tested-only+` |TESTED
|Checked on a bounded or sampled domain; bounds given.

|`+argued+` |ASSUMED
|A stated hypothesis, or a transcription no kernel has checked. Includes "the
CI gate exists and the authors say it passes" when no receipt is cited.

|`+open+` |OPEN
|Not done.
|===

=== Relation to the Mathesis grades

https://github.com/hyperpolymath/mathesis[mathesis], *Estate-wide Mathematical
Knowledge Architecture* §5, grades a *theorem* by how far its witness has
travelled: **Grade 3** type-checks locally in its home system and is not
translated; **Grade 2i/2ii** one / two independent translations with statement
correspondence recorded; **Grade 1** machine-checked through a verified checker
API in headless CI.

The two vocabularies are orthogonal — one grades a theorem's reach, the other
records whether a repository's obligation was discharged — but they interact:

* A home-system leg may be `+proven+` while every independent leg is `+open+`.
  That combination *is* Grade 3, and it is the ceiling for anything in this
  repository.
* A translation leg is `+argued+`, not `+proven+`, until the statement
  correspondence is recorded (`+PROOF-POLICY+` §2: "`the kernel checks the term
  only against the translated statement`").

Mathesis §6 forbids flattening assurance into a percentage. See R5 and R6.

=== Agda — theory and metatheory (§1, §2)

[width="100%",cols="24%,13%,38%,25%",options="header"]
|===
|Obligation |Row |Basis |Evidence / next step

|R4. Agda meta-checker: trust-level order, axiom safety, portfolio, dispatch,
axiom-tracker completeness, integrity, clamp bounds, receipt/warrant (9 modules)
|`+proven+`
|First-party Agda in `+meta-checker/src/+`, type-checked with the §2.1
canonical invocation. Receipt taken 2026-11-01 at commit `+916b769+`:

----
agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible \
  src/Echidna/MetaChecker.agda          # exit 0, 98 s cold
----

Versions: Agda `+2.7.0.1+`, `+agda-stdlib+` `+2.1+` (214 stdlib modules pulled
in). All 9 modules in `+src/Echidna/+` produce an interface; 53 warnings, all
of them `+-W[no]UnsupportedIndexedMatch+`, at five sites (`+TrustLevel+` 77,
88, 235; `+AxiomSafety+` 119; `+ClampTrust+` 98). No `+postulate+`, no
`+sorry+`, no `+believe_me+`, no `+Admitted+`; `+--safe+` is what excludes
them rather than a `+grep+`.
|Trust boundary, named: the Agda 2.7.0.1 kernel under `+--safe+`, plus the
stdlib. **Limit, recorded not hidden:** those warnings mean the flagged
functions do not *compute* under Cubical transports. The corpus is
`+--cubical-compatible+`, not `+--cubical+` — exactly the distinction
`+PROOF-POLICY+` §2.1 draws — and dropping the warning, or the flag, to get a
clean log would be the weakening §2.1 forbids. It is immaterial today because
nothing transports these terms; transport is R5's job, which is where the
limit would first bite.

**Why not just use `+--cubical+`?** Because it fails, and not on this corpus.
`+agda --cubical --safe src/Echidna/MetaChecker.agda+` exits non-zero in
`+agda-stdlib-2.1/src/Data/List/Membership/Setoid/Properties.agda+` with
unsolved metas, before any ECHIDNA module is reached: stdlib 2.1 is written to
be `+--cubical-compatible+`, and ships
`+--warning=noUnsupportedIndexedMatch+` in `+standard-library.agda-lib+` for
precisely this reason. So `+--cubical-compatible+` is not a weakened choice here;
it is the strongest flag this dependency set supports, and the policy's own
distinction between the two flags is what lets that be said plainly.

Promoted from `+argued+` on the strength of that receipt. Before it, the row
rested on "the CI gate exists and the authors say it passes" — which §5 defines
as `+argued+`, not a receipt. One module
(`+AxiomTrackerCompleteness.agda+`) turned out never to have been in the
checked closure at all, and did not type-check (see R9's note on scope).

|R9. E11 solver-integrity soundness
|`+proven+`
|`+meta-checker/src/Echidna/Integrity.agda+`, inside the R4 receipt. States the
obligation that had no Agda record: verification requires an initialised
manifest; verification means the recorded digest was matched; a substituted
binary is never verified; an uninitialised manifest never verifies. Collision
resistance is a record *field*, not a postulate, so the guarantee is
conditional and says so in its type; `+sharpness+` exhibits a comparator for
which that field cannot be inhabited, so the hypothesis is load-bearing rather
than decorative.
**Scope of the model (from review, 2026-11-01):** a binary is modelled by its
digest alone, so these are statements about *digests*, not bytes.
"Substituted" means "a binary whose digest differs", and two binaries colliding
on a digest are not distinguished by this model. Content-level collision
resistance sits in the `≈d-sound` field of the comparator — assumed, not
established. That is what makes the sharpness result the load-bearing part.

|**Specification, not implementation.** `+src/rust/dispatch.rs+` lines 642, 848
and 998, and `+src/rust/integrity/solver_integrity.rs+` line 359, all accept
`+IntegrityStatus::Uninitialized+` as passing. The theorem proves the opposite
of what that code does. That divergence is DEBT D9, now machine-checked on one
side instead of argued on both. Closing R9's implementation leg means changing
the Rust, not the proof.

|The Idris2 original stays *out of policy* per §3 and, as far as this

|R11. Epistemic separation: receipt vs warrant
|`+proven+`
|`+meta-checker/src/Echidna/Receipt.agda+`, inside the R4 receipt. A two-world
Kripke countermodel: a receipt is factive, a warrant is not; the same warrant
never licences the stronger token; while the agent cannot discriminate the
worlds, no receipt exists at all; and a second frame — same worlds, only the
accessibility changed — has a receipt, so what buys one is *excluding the
alternative*, not accumulating more evidence. (An earlier draft took the
discriminating check as a parameter; that was vacuous, since `≈-cross`
inhabits the domain of the function required. Two frames say it properly.)
This is what makes §5's "`+argued+` is not `+proven+`" a theorem instead of a

|Semantic rewrite of the receipt/warrant distinction from
https://github.com/hyperpolymath/epistemic-types[epistemic-types] at

this row is a rewrite of something that checks, not of an aspiration: per §2.2 the source project keeps
ownership of those, and this module proves only what ECHIDNA needs. The
residual limit is the honest one — a receipt requires a check that
*discriminates*, which is precisely the evidence R5 does not yet have.

== Not obligations

`+PROOF-POLICY+` §3: "`Corpora that exist to exercise provers are not
obligations. echidna's `+proofs/<prover>/+` trees and similar fixtures are test
material for a multi-prover system and are outside this policy.`"

So the sixteen `+proofs/<prover>/+` directories and `+training_data/+` carry no
row. They exercise parsers and backends. Listing them is deliberate: an absent
row should read as *out of policy*, not *forgotten*. The same holds for
`+archive/logtalk/+` (dead, quarantined) and for `+echidna-playground/+`.

Note the tension with R6 — one artefact in a corpus directory is cited by
`+PROOF-NEEDS+` as covering a real obligation. §3 is why that is a defect in
placement rather than a status question.

== What this file does not do

It is not a release gate and not a CI result. A `+proven+` row here would never
mean "ECHIDNA is verified" — it would mean one named obligation was discharged
by one named tool with its boundary written down. Nothing here vouches for a
prover backend's own soundness.

Four rows are `+proven+` today — R4, R9, R10, R11 — and each names the kernel,
the flags and the limit that qualify it (R4 carries the
`+UnsupportedIndexedMatch+` caveat, R9 the digest-level scope). Every *independent*
leg is still `+open+`: R5 has never been attempted, which is why the corpus sits
at Mathesis Grade 3 and not above it. Those two facts are not in tension —
proven in one kernel is exactly what Grade 3 means — and neither of them is an
omission.
