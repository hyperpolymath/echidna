= ADR: lexicon v2 (false friends, phrases, equivalences) and hub-and-probe identity arbitration (stage 6c)
:revdate: 2026-10-19
:status: proposed — owner decisions D1–D5 in §7 are open; nothing below is ruled

== Context (measured on `main` at `ed6ef60`, 2026-10-19)

The owner asked for two things at once: push the saturation levers again
(provers, corpora, vocabulary, synonyms) and go past lexical synonymy toward
*phrases*, *equivalent expressions*, *false friends*, and ultimately a way to
arbitrate proofs across prover families by establishing what mathematical
object each system is talking about — "feeling our way around mathematical
objects" rather than only checking the same text in many kernels.

What the tree already has:

* *Exchange bridges*: six (`src/rust/exchange/`: OpenTheory, Dedukti,
  Lambdapi, TPTP, SMT-LIB, SMTCoq). All work on rendered text. None produces a
  kernel-checked translation; `term_hash` is taken over the rendering, with no
  α-normalisation (roadmap 6c), so α-equivalent terms hash apart. Worse, the
  renderings are lossy: `term_to_opentheory` collapses every term outside
  Var / Const / App / Lambda to `const "echidna_opaque"` and drops binder
  types, so two different source terms can render, and hash, alike. Nothing
  fuses on these hashes today because dispatch attaches no evidence; once it
  does, a `KernelTranslation` may only come from an injective rendering of a
  supported fragment, with unsupported terms rejected, never collapsed (§5).
* *Arbitration*: four fusion mechanisms behind `ResultArbiter`, and since
  2026-10-14 (ECH-05, #430) a fidelity level `belief` / `epi` / `sound` set by
  per-attempt `FidelityEvidence` (`KernelTranslation` /
  `DeclaredCorrespondence` / `None`), with the Idris2 statement in
  `src/abi/EchidnaABI/Arbitration.idr`. Dispatch attaches no evidence yet, so
  every dispatched verdict is `belief`. The owner's first kernel-translation
  target is HOL4 ↔ HOL Light through OpenTheory (6a), then Lean 4 ↔ Rocq
  through Dedukti (6b). Issue #429 asks to *consume* fidelity evidence (fibre
  coverage, round trips, cross-prover agreement from one kernel plus
  independent standpoints) rather than collect N re-proofs.
* *Lexicon*: `data/synonyms/` holds 14 per-prover tables (343 `[[synonym]]`
  rows) and 4 cross-prover dictionaries (`_msc2020`, `_wordnet_math`,
  `_conceptnet_seed`, `_disciplines`; 262 rows). `semantic_class`, the only
  cross-prover join key, is present on 61 of the 343 per-prover rows, 40 of
  them in `mizar.toml`; nine tables have none. The schema can say "these names
  are interchangeable" and nothing else: no way to record a *false friend*
  (same name, different object), a *phrase* (a multi-token idiom that is one
  tactic or one concept), a *specialisation* (same theorem at a narrower
  type), or an *equivalence with a witness* (and which kind of witness).
* *Corpus*: `training_data/` is uneven. Over thirty provers have empty
  `premises_*` / `tactics_*` files; HOL Light alone contributes 328,825
  premise rows. `models/premise_vocab.txt` has 1,311,903 lines, which already
  exceeds the roadmap's "~1 M canonical tokens" target as a raw count; the
  metric that matters is `metrics/oov_rate.jl` (≤ 3 %), unmeasured since the
  saturation campaign.
* *Toolchain in the session container*: Rust only. No prover, no Julia, no
  Idris2. Everything measured below is lexical; no kernel was consulted.

=== Experiment 1 — the owner's first pair, HOL4 ↔ HOL Light

`scripts/false_friend_scan.sh training_data hol4 hol_light` (this PR;
deterministic, jq + coreutils, under a minute; rows with an empty name or
statement are dropped, which removes three HOL4 rows):

[cols="3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Quantity | Value
| HOL4 distinct (name, statement) rows / distinct names | 95,738 / 90,168
| HOL Light distinct (name, statement) rows / distinct names | 43,937 / 42,456
| Theorem names shared by both corpora | 5,060
| Shared-name statement pairs | 11,490
| … with equal statement text after whitespace normalisation (cognates) | 1,030
| … with differing statement text | 10,460
|===

Reading the differing pairs in order shows the dominant pattern is *not*
contradiction and *not* synonymy. It is type generalisation: HOL4's
`!f:real->real …` against HOL Light's `!f:real^M->real^N …`, `abs` against
`norm`, `0` against `vec 0` (HOL Light's multivariate library is the
n-dimensional generalisation of the shared ancestor). Those are
*specialisation* pairs: the HOL4 statement is an instance of the HOL Light
one. A lexicon with only `synonym` cannot express that, and an arbiter that
accepted them as "the same theorem" would be wrong in one direction and
right in the other. Genuine false friends also exist in the same list: the
name `lemma` carries unrelated statements in each corpus.

The Mizar figure is an artefact of the extractor keying on article-local
labels (`Th1`, `Th2`, …): 76,522 rows collapse onto 18,795 names, so the
corpus is nearly all homonyms *by construction*, and the low rate means the
extractor dropped most of them. Lean 3 and Why3 are genuinely namespace-heavy
(`add_comm` in dozens of structures). Both facts matter for anything that
joins corpora by name: a name is not an identity, even inside one prover.

=== What the numbers establish

. Lexical alignment is unsafe as *evidence*, which ECH-05 already assumes; the
  experiment quantifies how unsafe (nine in ten shared-name pairs differ).
. Lexical alignment is a high-yield *candidate generator*: 1,030
  whitespace-normalised cognates and roughly ten thousand specialisation
  candidates fall out of the first pair for free, before any kernel runs.
. The relation the lexicon most needs next is not more synonyms but
  `specialisation` / `generalisation`, then `false_friend`, then `phrase`.

== Options

Rated first for elegance and correctness, then for dependability and
security. Aspects noted where relevant.

=== A. Logical-framework hub (λΠ-modulo as the lingua franca)

Dedukti / Lambdapi as the single exchange target; prover-specific encoders do
the work (the Dedukti ecosystem ships them: OpenTheory → Dedukti, Coq →
Dedukti, Agda → Dedukti, Isabelle → Dedukti, Lean partial). Identity of two
statements is identity of their λΠ terms after normalisation, which the
arbiter already names as `KernelTranslation { term_hash }` and already proves
cannot fuse a collision to `sound`.

* Elegance / correctness: highest. One hub, kernel-checked, matches roadmap
  6b and #191 exactly, and the arbiter's `sound` level is already defined
  over it.
* Dependability / security: high once an encoder exists; each encoder is a
  research-grade artefact and the set of provers it covers is bounded.
* Aspect: this is the *only* route to `sound`. Everything else tops out at
  `epi`.

=== B. Behavioural identity by consequence partitions ("feeling our way around")

Identify an object by what it does, not by its term: fix a shared probe set
(consequences, instances, counter-instances), have each prover decide the
probes for its candidate statement, and call two candidates the same object
when they induce the same partition of the probe set. This is the standpoint
in `hyperpolymath/autoformal-fidelity` (two target standpoints; agreement is
identical partitions; convergence is evidence only under independence) and it
is what #429 asks ECHIDNA to consume. It is neurosymbolic in the intended
sense: the neural layer proposes alignments from corpus embeddings and
lexical candidates (Experiment 1); the symbolic layer tests them; the result
lands in the existing `DeclaredCorrespondence { consequence_tests, failures }`
constructor and yields `epi`, never `sound`.

* Elegance / correctness: high; reuses the ECH-05 vocabulary unchanged.
  Correctness is bounded by the probe set and by independence (same kernel,
  same library, same author all reduce it). The current
  `DeclaredCorrespondence { consequence_tests, failures }` has no place to
  carry independence, and `fidelity_level` lifts any tested, failure-free
  correspondence to `Epi`, so independence must travel inside the evidence:
  `DeclaredCorrespondence` gains a standpoint record (kernel, library and
  authorship of each side), `fidelity_level` lifts to `Epi` only when the
  standpoints differ, and the `Arbitration.idr` constructor takes the same
  argument. That is an additive arbiter and ABI change, and the one this
  proposal needs.
* Dependability / security: high. Probes are ordinary prover invocations and
  run inside the existing sandbox and integrity checks.
* Aspect: this is the route that handles *false friends* natively (different
  partitions) and *specialisation* (one partition refines the other), neither
  of which a term hash can express.

=== C. An in-house super-language or universal IR

A new estate language in which every prover's statements are written.
Rated lowest: it reinvents MMT / Dedukti, needs the same per-prover encoders
as A, and adds a kernel nobody has checked. The useful kernel of the idea is
already in the tree: `core::Term` in `crates/echidna-core/src/core.rs` is a
small dependent-type IR (Var, Const, App, Lambda, Pi, Sigma, universes, Let).
The elegant move is to make `core::Term` the α-normalised *projection target*
that renders to λΠ (6c), not to invent a language around it. KYAML, deed and
System-ET are serialisation surfaces, not logics: they can carry the lexicon
and the certificates, and they cannot carry identity.

=== D. Pairwise translators (Mathport-style)

Lean 3 → Lean 4, HOL Light → HOL4 via OpenTheory, and so on. Pragmatic where
a mature tool exists; quadratic in the number of provers; keeps every pair as
its own special case. Kept as *feeders* into A, never as the architecture.

=== E. Identity as a VeriSimDB consonance subject (alternative framing, cross-repo)

`hyperpolymath/verisimdb` already defines exactly this problem for data:
one identity maintained across modal witnesses, with drift detection and the
verbs `MERGE` / `SPLIT` / `RETRACT`. Treat each prover as a modal witness of
one mathematical object: a cognate is consonance, a false friend is drift,
curation is `MERGE` or `SPLIT`, retraction of a backend
(`ResultArbiter::retract`) is `RETRACT`. This is not a competitor to A + B;
it is where the identity lives between runs, and it is the estate trope the
owner asked to look for. It belongs in verisimdb, not here.

== Recommendation

*Hub and probe*: A for `sound` where an encoder exists (6a OpenTheory on the
owner's HOL4 ↔ HOL Light pair first, 6b Dedukti next), B for `epi` everywhere
else, D as feeders, C rejected, E as the persistence layer, filed against
verisimdb. The hybrid is the elegant option because it maps onto the two
evidence constructors ECH-05 already proves properties about: the fusion
mechanisms are untouched, and the arbiter and ABI changes it needs are
additive (the standpoint record on `DeclaredCorrespondence`, option B, and
the injectivity requirement on `KernelTranslation` renderings, option A).
It departs from nothing the owner has ruled.

== Decision (proposed; lands only after D1–D5)

. *Lexicon v2 schema*, additive and loader-compatible (serde defaults), on
  every `[[synonym]]` row:
  * `relation`: one of `synonym` (default), `specialisation`,
    `generalisation`, `false_friend`, `phrase`.
  * `phrase`: ordered token list for a multi-token idiom (for example
    Mizar's `then thus`, Coq's `apply … ; auto`, Isabelle's `by (simp add:`).
  * `false_friends`: names that *look* like this entry in other tables and
    are not it, with the other table named.
  * `evidence`: `none` (default), `curated`, `probe { tests, failures }`,
    `kernel { format, checked_by, term_hash }`. `curated` records provenance
    only and lifts to `FidelityEvidence::None` until probe or kernel evidence
    is attached; `probe` lifts to `DeclaredCorrespondence`, and `kernel` to
    `KernelTranslation` only when every field, `checked_by` included, is
    present. A lexicon row therefore never raises an attempt's fidelity
    above what its own evidence carries.
  * `fidelity`: `exact` or `lossy` with a `residue` note, after echo-types'
    loss-with-residue formalism, so a lossy correspondence says what is lost.
  * `scope`: library and version the relation holds in (the existing `since`
    / `until` cover the prover version only).
. *Candidate generation from corpora*: `scripts/false_friend_scan.sh` now; a
  Rust `echidna lexicon scan` later that emits v2 rows with
  `evidence = none`. Candidates never set `semantic_class` on their own.
. *Consequence probes*: a `verification/consequence_probe.rs` that takes a
  probe set per pair, runs it through the existing dispatcher and sandbox,
  and produces `DeclaredCorrespondence` with its standpoint record;
  `fidelity_level` and `Arbitration.idr` are extended in the same PR so that
  correlated probes cannot reach `Epi`. First probe
  pair: the SMT-LIB pair Z3 ↔ CVC5, because both binaries already install in
  `live-provers.yml` Tier 1, so the probe loop is testable in CI today.
. *Hub*: 6a real OpenTheory (article reader and writer against the real
  stack-machine format, compatible with the OpenTheory → Dedukti encoder),
  with the CR-8 round-trip test from `standards`
  `3-practice/testing-and-benchmarking/TESTING-TAXONOMY.adoc`.
. *6c*: α-normalised projection of `core::Term` to λΠ; `term_hash` over the
  canonical form; the renderer rejects terms outside the supported fragment
  instead of collapsing them to an opaque constant; property tests that
  α-equivalent terms hash equal and that distinct terms in the fragment never
  share a hash.
. *Dispatch attaches evidence* (closes "every verdict is `belief`"), then
  #429's ingestion.

== Beyond this repository

* `hyperpolymath/verisimdb`: map cross-prover object identity onto a
  consonance subject (option E); one issue to open.
* `hyperpolymath/standards`: CR-8 names the round-trip pairs; the FRG grades'
  "cross-prover replication ≥ 2 / ≥ 3" (V2 / V3) should require `epi` or
  better, not lexical agreement, once this lands.
* `hyperpolymath/autoformal-fidelity` and `hyperpolymath/nextgen-typing`
  (`docs/TYPE-CONNECTIONS.adoc`): the partition-agreement statement B
  implements; cite, do not re-derive.
* `hyperpolymath/echo-types`: the residue of a lossy correspondence
  (`fidelity = lossy`) is an echo-types L3 object; the lexicon should cite the
  loss taxonomy rather than invent names for kinds of loss.
* `hyperpolymath/echidnabot`: consumes `echidna.prove.result/1`; the
  verdict's `level` should surface in its trust bridge once dispatch attaches
  evidence.
