Group matching declarations by their *structural* identity key.

The key is the SHA-256 of the type's canonical shape — variable names, module paths, operator spellings and binder keywords are normalised away — so `+-comm` in Agda, `Nat.add_comm` in Lean and `eq Nat (add x y) (add y x)` in Dedukti land on one key with no synonym lookup. `--skeleton` additionally prints the binder- and sort-free key, which is what provers that elide binders (Isabelle) match on.

Usage: echidna corpus identity [OPTIONS] <PATTERN>

Arguments:
  <PATTERN>
          Name pattern to match: a substring of the qualified or short name, case-insensitive. `*` matches every declaration

Options:
      --format <FORMAT>
          Output format (text, json)
          
          [default: text]

  -i, --index <INDEX>
          Path to the corpus JSON. Defaults to `data/corpus/<basename>.json` for the cwd's basename

      --skeleton
          Also print the skeleton key (binders and sorts erased)

  -v, --verbose
          Verbose output

      --cross-only
          Only show keys that group declarations from more than one adapter

      --no-color
          Disable colors

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')
